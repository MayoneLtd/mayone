This Privacy Policy specifically governs the mobile application NOAA Weather: Live Radar & Alerts ("NOAA Weather", the "App"), developed and published by Mayone Co., Ltd. ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), a company registered under the laws of Vietnam.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparent data practices. This policy details how our application handles location data, device diagnostics, and optional subscriptions in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies (specifically User Data and Location Data policies), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Location Data Collection & Use (Prominent Disclosure)

To provide accurate local atmospheric data, high-resolution Doppler radar maps, and life-saving severe storm warnings, NOAA Weather requests permission to access your device's geographical location:

  • (a) Approximate and Precise Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION & ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): When you grant location access, the App uses your coordinates solely while the App is in use to fetch local radar tiles from the nearest National Weather Service (NOAA) Doppler station, pinpoint current temperature, display local precipitation probabilities, and notify you of nearby flash flood, hurricane, or tornado warnings.
  • (b) Strict Privacy Protections for Location Data:
    • We do NOT track your location continuously in the background for advertising purposes.
    • We do NOT sell, license, or share your precise location coordinates with data brokers or marketing intermediaries.
    • Location coordinates are transmitted over encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections directly to atmospheric weather API servers solely for the fulfillment of weather forecast queries.
    • You may deny location permissions at any time through your Android system settings and manually search and bookmark cities by name or postal code without granting GPS permissions.

2. Other Information We Collect & Permissions

  • (a) Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used exclusively to deliver time-sensitive severe weather alerts, lightning proximity warnings, and daily weather forecast summaries. You can toggle or customize alert categories at any time in the in-app Settings menu.
  • (b) Device Diagnostics & Crash Reports: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as Android OS version, device model, crash logs, and aggregated screen interaction metrics) is collected via Firebase Crashlytics to detect software bugs and optimize map rendering speed.
  • (c) In-App Purchases (BILLING): Premium subscriptions (ad-free experience, extended forecasts, and specialized hurricane layers) are processed directly and securely by the Google Play In-App Billing system. We never access or store your financial payment credentials.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our application integrates trusted third-party SDKs that comply strictly with Google Play security and privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services & In-App Billing: Handles app distribution, authentication, and secure digital purchases. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Crashlytics: Collects aggregated, anonymized telemetry to help our engineering team diagnose performance bottlenecks and crashes.
  • NOAA & Open Atmospheric APIs: Public meteorological data feeds utilized to render radar animations, satellite layers, and storm track polygons.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

NOAA Weather is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at mayoneltd2026@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete any associated records.

5. Data Retention, Security & User Rights

We apply modern industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit. Because your saved favorite locations and custom weather settings are stored locally on your device, you can erase all local data instantly by clearing the app data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.
