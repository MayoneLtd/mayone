01 Overview & Developer Statement

This Privacy Policy describes how developer Nguyen Phan Ngoc Anh ("we", "us", or "our") collects, protects, uses, and discloses information gathered from users of the mobile application Go.Color (Package ID: com.mayone.pixelartpaint).

We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This application is designed to provide relaxing pixel art coloring and creative entertainment without compromising your personal privacy. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Verification Notice: This privacy policy strictly applies to the Google Play Store distribution of Go.Color published under the developer account of Nguyen Phan Ngoc Anh.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Go.Color, we may process the following types of information:

  • Non-Personal Usage & Analytical Data: Aggregated, anonymous statistics such as session duration, coloring artwork completion counts, app feature usage, and UI interactions to improve app performance and user experience.
  • Device & Technical Identifiers (Including Google Advertising ID): Device model, operating system version (Android), unique anonymous instance identifiers, crash logs, and Google Advertising ID (AAID / AdServices Attribution) collected automatically via Firebase Analytics to measure app stability, campaign attribution, and fix bugs. Users can reset or delete their Advertising ID anytime in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • In-App Purchases (VIP Subscriptions & Coins): Transactions are handled directly by Google Play In-App Billing. We never collect or store your payment card numbers or banking information on our servers.
  • Push Notification Tokens (FCM): If you grant the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission, a random Firebase Cloud Messaging token is generated to send daily pixel art updates, daily bonuses, and event reminders. You can revoke notification permissions at any time in device settings.
  • User Generated Artworks: Coloring progress, completed pixel art creations, and local favorites are stored locally on your device storage unless backup is explicitly chosen.

03 How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To operate, deliver, and maintain the functionality of Go.Color.
  • To save your coloring progress, unlocked badges, and virtual achievements.
  • To analyze app performance, investigate crashes, and roll out bug fixes and optimizations.
  • To verify valid purchases and unlock VIP content through Google Play Billing.
  • To comply with Google Play Developer Program Policies and applicable legal regulations.

05 Device Permissions

The application only requests device permissions that are strictly necessary to perform its functions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch remote artwork catalogs, verify Google Play licenses, and send crash analytics.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / PHOTO PICKER (Optional): Used solely if you choose to import a personal photo to convert it into pixel artwork inside the "Create" mode. We do not upload your personal photos to private cloud servers without consent.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide tactile haptic feedback during pixel coloring (can be disabled in Settings).

06 Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

In compliance with the Google Play User Data Policy, we provide users with full control over their data:

How to Request Data Deletion: If you wish to delete any analytical records or data associated with your installation of Go.Color, please submit a deletion request to developer Nguyen Phan Ngoc Anh via email at npngocanh228@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Go.Color". Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

You can also reset all locally stored artwork and app state at any time by clearing the application data in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Go.Color > Storage > Clear Data).

07 Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Go.Color is designed as a family-friendly coloring application suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to remove such information from our records.

08 Data Security

We employ modern industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, to protect data transmitted between the app and backend services. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% impenetrable.
