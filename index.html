About Us

Technology with purpose.

Mayone Co., Ltd. is an independent software company focused on building thoughtful, well-crafted digital products. We design and develop apps for iOS, macOS, and the web — tools that solve real problems with elegant, intuitive interfaces.

We believe great software comes from small, focused teams who care deeply about every pixel and every line of code. That philosophy drives everything we ship.

What We Do

Our areas of expertise

We specialize in end-to-end product development — from concept and design to engineering and release.

Mobile Development

Native iOS and cross-platform mobile applications with premium UI/UX, built with Swift, SwiftUI, and modern architectures.

Desktop Software

Powerful macOS applications designed for productivity, creativity, and daily workflows — respecting native platform conventions.

UI/UX Design

Human-centered design with attention to detail — fluid animations, accessible layouts, and interfaces that feel alive.

Web Applications

Responsive web platforms and tools — from corporate websites to full-stack SaaS applications with modern frameworks.
